Episode 2

English Translation

"Are you sure you're feeling all right?"

The girl was still peering at me with that worried look. The other two had vanished the moment they found out I was a resident here.

"Yeah, uh-huh." Her face was so close that my heart skipped a little as I answered. "I haven't eaten anything since this morning... and, you know, the hill up to this place is brutal."

"You climbed all the way up from the station!?"

Guess that really was a crazy thing to do. Yeah, normal people take the bus.

A door opened down the hall. Room 201, according to the plate.

The man from before poked his head out and—

"You've got guts, kid. Here, a little reward."

—came out holding a big bottle of sake in one hand and a bag of snacks in the other, and plonked them down on the wooden floor.

Huh?

"Nike-san (Yukiko's name for him), Taro-san only just got here. And he was passed out until a minute ago."

"He just said he hasn't eaten a thing, didn't he? Eat, eat."

He pushed the snacks at me. Dried squid, more dried squid, tiny fish... nothing that would actually fill a stomach.

Then the door on the opposite side opened, and the woman from before came out.

"Here, I was saving this for a snack, but—"

—she held up a convenience-store pork cutlet bowl.

"Want it?"

A pork cutlet bowl, for a snack...? A little suspicious, but my stomach answered on its own. The woman put a hand to her mouth, giggled, said she'd warm it up, and ducked back inside.

Embarrassed, I glanced at the girl. She was laughing too. Dammit.

Before I knew it, the man had already started drinking.

"Nike-san! You promised you wouldn't drink in the middle of the day!"

"A welcome party doesn't count, does it? Right, kid? Sake's a nutrient, isn't it!"

"I'm a minor, actually."

"What minor doesn't drink?"

This guy is hopeless.

"No, it's just, my parents are strict..."

Back in high school, my parents absolutely refused to let me drink. Truth is, I did sneak a drink exactly once, at a friend's house—but my old man sniffed out the alcohol, beat the crap out of me, and it turned into a full-on fistfight anyway. It's not that I was scared of him; it's just that every drink turning into that was such a pain that I never touched the stuff again.

Not that I thought my old man was being unreasonable. Running a hot spring inn means our place hosts banquets practically every night. Our family's rooms were a little ways off, but there wasn't a single night you couldn't hear the drunks. Plenty of guests caused trouble, too, so my old man was seriously strict about alcohol. Even when he drank himself, he was careful never to let the kids see him looking pathetic.

So... wait a second. I'm not in Oita anymore.

I don't have to get into fistfights with my old man anymore!

Maybe he sensed something from the change in my expression, because the old guy held out an empty cup.

"Call it climbing the stairway to adulthood."

I took it nervously, and he filled it to the brim with sake.

"You don't have to drink it if you don't want to, all right? I'll give him a good scolding for you."

The girl—Yuki-chan-san? Miss Yuki-chan?—said that for me, but I was a free man now!

I threw it back in one gulp.

"Whoa, whoa, not bad, kid. Can't let you outdo me."

So saying, the old guy poured himself another cup.

Just then, the woman came back out of her room, carrying the pork cutlet bowl and a plastic bag with something sake-like inside.

"Oh my, oh my, you've already started."

"Natsuko-san, not you too..."

"Why not? I do love this sort of thing. Juice for you, Yuki-chan?"

"Ah, yes please."

Yuki-chan seemed like the type who easily gets swept along.

"Now then. Yuki-chan, I didn't set this whole thing up just because I wanted a drink, you know."

Still sitting cross-legged, the old guy puffed out his chest like he was putting on airs.

"I still haven't accepted this guy yet."

Huh?

"But Nike-san, you were so happy when you heard Taro-san was coming."

"That's one thing, this is another."

"Um—" I cut in without thinking.

"Does everybody here already know my name?"

The three of them looked at each other.

"Ah! Right, right, the landlady told us."

Yuki-chan said it like she was covering for something.

The landlady?

"Don't sweat the small stuff, kid."

"No, wait, if the old guy was thrilled to hear I was moving in, that is something I'd want to know."

"Small stuff! It's all small stuff, kid! I'll tell you once we're closer."

"Meaning...?"

"Drink more."

So he did just want an excuse to drink.

I looked to the woman for help, but she only smiled mysteriously and clearly wasn't going to tell me anything.

"So, kid—" —the old guy asked, swirling his cup. "You were passed out in the entryway. What happened?"

I groaned and pressed my forehead.

"...Well, the thing is, I lost my wallet at Tokyo Station."

"What!?" —Yuki-chan gasped.

"Then how did you get here?"

"I borrowed money at the police box."

"Don't people use that thing nowadays? What's it called—cashless?"

"I don't own a cell phone."

The three of them looked at each other again.

"Just to check—you are from Japan, of this world, right?"

"For the record, Kyushu does have cell phones. My parents just never let me have one."

"Hey, hey, I'm not making fun of you. Right?"

When the old guy threw it to them, the other two nodded.

"Learning how to use mine was a real struggle, you know." —said the woman.

"I'm still hopeless with mine." —said Yuki-chan.

"Well, don't let it get you down. I'm broke half the year myself."

Come to think of it, today was a weekday. Was this old guy maybe... unemployed?

"Um, it was Nike-san, right?"

"Hold it. Before you ask—I'm self-employed, got it?"

"Doing what?"

"Uh... resolving disputes, that kind of thing?"

"So, unemployed?"

"Shut it. I do get work. Sometimes."

"Since we're at it, let's do introductions." —said the woman. "I'm Sugaya Natsuko. I'm a housewife, so does that make me unemployed too?"

"Ha ha ha! You hear that? You just called Natsuko-san unemployed."

"I did not! And being a housewife is not unemployment!"

"Yuki-chan's unemployed too, you know."

"I am not!" —protested Yuki-chan. "I start university next week!"

University?

"Taro-san! I'm Hisame Yukiko! I'm entering MushaDai!"

"MushaDai? Wait, that's..."

"The same university!"

Seriously?

Can this really be happening?

I move all the way to Tokyo, and in the very place I'm going to live, there's a girl entering the same university?

Are we going to commute together?

Just the two of us?

Oh, man. Choosing Sakura Saku Dorm was the right call. So the right call.

"What's with that face? Hey, Yuki-chan—if this guy jumps you, come straight to my room."

"Old man!"

"Who're you calling an old man!?"

Ignoring the indignant old guy, I looked at Yuki-chan... no, at Hisame-san.

Uh oh, her smile was going stiff.

"I'm not some kind of creep, okay! I just... thought maybe we could be friends!"

"Don't believe him—men are wolves."

The old guy piped up from behind me.

"...Was your husband a wolf too, Natsuko-san?"

Yuki-chan asked the woman beside her, looking worried. Natsuko-san went 'oh my, oh my' and put a hand to her mouth.

"Well now... He was very passionate. He knew he'd be punished, and still, he carried me off and..."

"Carried you off!?"

Some outrageous story was about to start, so I put a stop to it.

"By the way... there are other people living here, right?"

"Oh, right, I forgot." The old guy drained his cup and pointed at one corner of the hall.

"Room 103, over there. Never go near it, got it?"

"Never?" I had no intention of bothering anyone in their own room anyway, but was even going near it off-limits?

"No, don't get me wrong. It's not that I suspect you'd do anything. The person living there is unwell, that's all."

"Is it... COVID?"

"It's not an infection or anything like that, so don't worry about that. It's just, their temper..."

"A difficult person?"

"Just feverish and irritable. Your room's the farthest one away, so as long as you live normally, you'll be fine."

"Once the fever's gone, I'll introduce you properly." —said Yuki-chan. "She's a good kid."

From the way she said it, maybe a girl around my age?

"There are a few more residents, and you'll run into them sooner or later." —said the old guy. "Just say hello when you do."

"Hey, Yuki-chan. Didn't you say you wanted to go see the university before term starts?" —said Natsuko-san.

"Yes. But everyone keeps stopping me..."

"Why not go with Taro-kun? Nobody would object to that, right, everyone?"

"Really!?"

Hisame-san's—Yukiko-san's—Yuki-san's?—eyes lit up. What was I even supposed to call her?

"Taro-san, won't you take me!?"

"S-sure, I don't mind..."

I had no idea why she'd been stopped from going alone. She wasn't a little kid or anything.

More importantly—a trip to the university, just the two of us! I'd barely moved in and already luck was on my side.

If we get close before term starts, maybe I'll even end up with a beautiful girlfriend...

"But listen, kid—" —the old guy said, pouring sake into my cup. "The university's a train ride from here, right? You got money for that?"

Don't remind me of the one thing I didn't want to think about.

But he was right. University life was the least of my problems.

I didn't have money to eat tonight, and worst of all, if I couldn't pay next month's rent, I'd be thrown out.

No, wait. That's right—my bankbook!

With my bankbook and my seal, I could withdraw money from my account! I explained it to them in a panic, confirmed there was a branch nearby, and dashed out of the dorm.

Why hadn't I thought of it sooner? This would solve everything... for now, at least.

But reality wasn't that kind.

To begin with, my account only held a bit of living money and next month's rent... and somehow, that account was now at zero.

"If the account was used fraudulently, we can freeze your cash card for you."

That was all the clerk said, and all I could do was nod.

Some heartless bastard had picked up my wallet and taken what little money I had.

Ahh... what now?

For today, at least... ah, dammit. To go back and sulk in bed, I'd have to climb that hill again...

Will I even make it to university life?
