Episode 5 The Door to Nowhere

English Translation

I woke with a splitting headache.

...What had happened?

In a daze, I sat up, piecing together fragments of memory. The clock by my pillow said 9 PM. I hadn't slept that long. And yet, something was wrong.

Pressing my temples, I tried to remember—there had been something on the roof. A giant silhouette floating in the moonlight. Like... a dragon? No, that couldn't be. I must have been so tired I'd hallucinated it.

Still half-doubting, I staggered to the window and looked out. The roof—nothing there. Just a quiet night sky.

"A dream... right?" —I muttered, rubbing my temples. "I was just exhausted, that's all..."

But the dorm's unnatural silence kept nagging at me. It wasn't late enough for everyone to be asleep. The faint sound of a TV, floorboards creaking, Nike grumbling about something—there should have been something. But there was nothing.

Maybe everyone in this dorm just slept well. Still... it was kind of creepy.

I went out into the hallway. The creaking stairs sounded unnaturally loud. I knocked on Natsuko-san's door.

...No answer.

Had she gone to the bathhouse? Resigned, I knocked on Nike's door.

...Silence.

No answer. Had he gone to the bathhouse too?

A bad feeling crawled up my spine. "Anyone there?" —I called out.

There was no reply.

My chest tightened. This silence wasn't just a vague wrongness—something was off.

Natsuko-san and Nike weren't at the bathhouse. I had that feeling.

I stood in front of Yuki-chan's room and, hesitating, knocked.

...Still nothing.

Dammit, what was going on?

The people in this dorm had been strange from the very start. The weird looks from the neighbors. The way the residents—who should have been meeting me for the first time—acted as if they'd known me all along. The vague explanations, the ambiguous warnings—nobody would tell me anything in detail. And Yuki-chan's reaction yesterday—her excessive response when Nike's work came up, and how shockingly naive she was about the world—now, all of it seemed 'not normal.'

All the little wrongnesses I'd been trying to ignore could no longer be ignored.

In the end, I still wasn't recognized as a resident of this dorm.

"...This dorm is definitely strange."

I muttered under my breath.

And then—I noticed.

Room 103's door was slightly open.

A cold chill raced up my spine.

Nike had told me never to go near that room.

But now—it was open.

I hesitated. I should have walked away right then. But at that moment—from inside came a faint, pained groan.

Someone was in there.

I swallowed hard. "...Is someone there?"

No answer.

I put my hand on the door frame and stepped inside.

In the darkness, on the tatami, a girl lay collapsed. A small child, still elementary-school age. Her skin was pale, sweat beading on her forehead. A terrible fever.

"Dammit..." I muttered, and knelt beside her. "Hey, are you all right?"

The girl groaned faintly, but she seemed barely conscious.

Nike had said she was 'sick,' but this was... bad. In her condition, with not even a futon—she'd only get worse.

"Wait right here." I turned to the closet. There should be a spare futon in there.

Just as I reached to open it—

"...No..." —the girl murmured in a hoarse voice.

"Huh?"

"Don't... open it..."

She was clearly just delirious. I ignored her and opened the closet.

But there was no futon inside.

Instead—there was a door.

An out-of-place, uncanny door. Intricate patterns carved into dark wood. Entwined dragon carvings. An aged brass knob.

"What is this...?"

"If you open it... you can't come back..."

The girl's voice echoed faintly.

I couldn't make sense of what she was saying at all.

I stared at the door. For some reason, every hair on my body stood on end. In the faint light, the carvings seemed to writhe. The air around me felt unnaturally heavy, dense.

—I mustn't open it.

My instincts screamed it.

I absolutely should not open it. A door inside a closet? I'd bet my life it was nothing good.

But beyond this door—I felt the answers to every strange thing so far were waiting.

The reason I couldn't become a resident of this dorm—felt like it was there.

So I—wanted to see for myself. Even if it led to something irreversible—when that happened, I'd deal with it then. Move out, go back home, whatever it took.

I grabbed the doorknob.

In that instant, the door opened on its own.

Blinding light flooded my vision.

A powerful force dragged me in.

With no time to resist—I was swallowed by what lay beyond.

I was slammed into the ground.

"Ngh... guh..."

My whole body ached. Dry earth, a gritty feel. Wind struck my face.

Where was I—?

I forced my aching body up and slowly looked around.

It was an endless expanse of hills. The sky was dyed a pale purple, and the wind was strangely clear. An unfamiliar, mysterious scent drifted in the air.

I slowly turned around.

In the distance towered a fantasy castle, like something out of a game or anime. Tall towers, massive walls, flags fluttering in the wind.

On the other side, a volcano burning bright red.

Tokyo was nowhere to be found.

The dorm, the door—gone.

"...Where the hell am I...?"
